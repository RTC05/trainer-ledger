Privacy

Privacy and account data

Trainer Ledger stores only the data needed to run your account, save your progress, and deliver core site features.

What we store
  • Username and hashed password
  • Optional recovery email
  • Per-game progress, notes, and V2 state
  • Suggestions and bug reports you submit
What we do not ask for
  • No credit card information on Trainer Ledger itself
  • No software downloads to use the tracker
  • No bank details or government IDs
Support and third parties

Support links may send you to Patreon or other external providers. Those services have their own privacy policies and account systems.
