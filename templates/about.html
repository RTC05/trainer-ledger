About

What Trainer Ledger is

Trainer Ledger is a fan-made Pokemon tracker and guide hub built to help players plan full runs, track regional dex progress, and keep route-important catches, items, secrets, and one-time decisions in one place.

Project status

Trainer Ledger is a live public beta. The guides are usable now, but the product is still being actively refined, expanded, and hardened for broader public use.

What the site does
  • Guide-first V2 pages for full playthrough support
  • Regional dex tracking before National Dex unlocks
  • Per-user progress, notes, and account recovery
  • Fan support through Patreon
Affiliation

Trainer Ledger is fan-made and is not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak, Creatures Inc., or The Pokémon Company.
